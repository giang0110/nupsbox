# P3.16 Premium Brand Experience

Date: 2026-09-19

## Goal
Move the public homepage from a component-heavy SaaS aesthetic toward a more premium, editorial and trustworthy storage brand experience.

## Changes
- Editorial hero with larger real facility imagery, shorter headline and one dominant CTA.
- Secondary hero action becomes a text link instead of a competing button.
- Replace icon/badge-heavy trust copy with a simple three-column fact strip.
- Rewrite gallery messaging in customer-facing language.
- Replace the card-heavy proof bento with a restrained trust story.
- Simplify the location/rental journey into one facility block and a numbered three-step flow.
- Upgrade final CTA to navy premium treatment.
- Strengthen footer brand presentation and clarify enquiry-time pricing/availability.
- Quiet the header navigation by reducing shadows and pill-like navigation chrome.

## Guardrails
- No database migrations.
- No auth, RLS or permission changes.
- No invented facility features, prices, availability or operating hours.
- Existing conversion tracking and CTA intents remain intact.
- Existing CMS gallery remains the source of public facility images.
